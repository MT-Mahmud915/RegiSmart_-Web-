# RegiSmart – University Examination Registration and Admit Card Management System

## Overview

RegiSmart is a web-based University Examination Registration and Admit Card Management System designed to simplify the examination registration process for university students. The system allows students to register for courses before examinations, complete payment, and generate their admit cards after successful registration.

The project aims to make examination registration more efficient, organized, and accessible by reducing manual processes and managing student registration information digitally.

## Features

- **Course-Wise Registration:** Students can select and register for their examination courses.
- **Student Information Management:** Manage student details and registration information.
- **Payment System:** Facilitate payment for examination registration.
- **Registration Confirmation:** Confirm registration after successful completion of required steps.
- **Admit Card Generation:** Generate admit cards for students after completing the registration process.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Provide an easy-to-use interface for students to complete their registration.

## Technologies Used

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js
- **Backend:** Node.js
- **Other Tools:** Git, GitHub

## System Workflow

1. Students access the registration portal.
2. Select their required examination courses.
3. Complete the registration and payment process.
4. The system confirms successful registration.
5. Students generate and download their admit cards.

## Project Objectives

- Digitize the university examination registration process.
- Simplify course-wise examination registration.
- Reduce manual administrative work.
- Provide a convenient payment and registration process.
- Automate admit card generation after successful registration.
